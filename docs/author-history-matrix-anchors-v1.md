# Phase 11 · Matrix-backed historical identity anchors

The ten remaining Latin American authors with curated histories now have a local documentary fallback, complementing Antonio Caso's existing one. The selected titles are exact entries in the canonical dossier; variants within a group are not independent works. Each candidate requires at least two distinct groups and matching creator fields, and cannot override explicit QIDs or ORCIDs. All ten QIDs were checked against public Wikidata item names on 2026-11-27. This does not reverify the underlying biographical claims. No catalog records or statistics are modified. The data is a small reviewed crosswalk, not a second bibliography. Tests check real dossier titles, identity bridge, conflicting identifiers, title duplication and archive link. Five original fallback candidates plus ten new candidates = 15 of 30 curated authors supported by this fallback; explicit QIDs remain supported for all 30. Remaining 15 require further research. The PR is a draft independent of #407 to avoid interrupting its ongoing end-to-end run; merge only after #407 is validated and these tests pass.
