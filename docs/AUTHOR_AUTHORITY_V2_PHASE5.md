## Phase 5B — exact archive links and scoped analytical projection

The preview now relates document groups to existing archive cards using exact DOI or provider/source identifiers, requiring a unique match in both directions. No title-only linkage, modification of saved IDs, or new record persistence. Multiple matches remain ambiguous; no exact match remains unresolved. The canonical modal opens only the original matched work object. Shadow-only records keep their evidence and DOI when present. Cards display a preview label but are not hidden or rewritten.

Separate authorship counts from participation roles. Only explicitly author-role confirmed/probable groups contribute to the V2 chronology, as separate counts; ambiguous/rejected and other-role records do not. Conflicting or absent years are reported separately, historical signed years are retained, no invented months. Coauthor candidates are based only on identified creators of a source record with a confirmed author-role contribution, never merged metadata or synthesized snapshot creators; their identities are not independently confirmed. Legacy metrics and chronology are still untouched, not authoritative V2 output. No network claims when corroborated evidence is absent. The preview revision is a configured reference, not proof that every runtime source carries that revision.

Tests cover exact linkage, collisions, preserved object identity, role separation, date conflict and limited coauthor evidence. Browser test exercises canonical callback and evaluated chronology. The full theme matrix, true end-to-end author retrieval preview, verified human identity projection and production rollout remain pending. Phase 5A CI was absent at the time of this update; neither 5A nor 5B is declared validated until checks run for the new head.
