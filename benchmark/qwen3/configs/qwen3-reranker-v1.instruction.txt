Given an academic philosophy search query, retrieve documents that substantively address the complete information need.

Treat explicit authors, works, philosophical concepts, and disciplinary domains as required constraints unless the query clearly expresses an alternative.

For interdisciplinary queries, require a substantive connection between the philosophical topic and the external domain. A document that substantively addresses only one side is insufficient.

Do not reward mere keyword overlap, incidental mentions, journal names, publisher metadata, retrieval provenance, or the fact that a document was retrieved by a particular query variant.

Judge relevance only from the supplied query and document content.
