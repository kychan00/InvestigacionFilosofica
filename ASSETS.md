Wikimedia Commons describes the source file as a faithful photographic
reproduction of a two-dimensional public-domain work of art. The Commons file
page states that the original work is in the public domain because Raphael died
in 1520, and identifies the digital file as free of known restrictions under
copyright law, including related and neighboring rights.

The project's root MIT License is therefore not the source of permission to
reuse this image. The image is included on the basis of its documented
public-domain status.

The underlying artwork is unquestionably public domain. Wikimedia Commons also

that it is free of known copyright restrictions. Commons notes, however, that

differently. This provenance record therefore preserves the exact Commons
